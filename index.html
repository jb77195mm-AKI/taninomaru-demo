Why Taninomaruたにの丸について

Two boats. Two captains.
One fishing family.二隻の船と、親子二代の船長。

Taninomaru is run by a father-and-son team out of Shioya Port, Gobo. The son, Captain Koki, skippers the lure boat — casting, jigging, tairaba. His father runs the classic bait boat, Tanino-maru. Whatever style of fishing you dream of, one of our boats is rigged for it.たにの丸は御坊市塩屋港の親子二代の遊漁船。若船長こうきのルアー船（キャスティング・ジギング・タイラバ）と、父の谷野丸（エサ釣り）の二隻体制。やりたい釣りに合わせてご案内します。

Absolutely — "beginners very welcome" is literally our motto. The captain and crew will rig your tackle, show you the technique, and even unhook your fish. Many of our happiest photos are first-timers.もちろんです。「初心者大歓迎」がモットー。仕掛けのセットから釣り方、魚の外し方までスタッフがサポートします。

No license is required for recreational boat fishing in Japan. Just book, board, and fish.日本では遊漁船での釣りに免許は不要です。

Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, rain jacket, food & drinks (fridge/microwave/hot water on board), and a cooler or bag for your catch — we provide the ice free. Seasickness medicine the night before + morning of is strongly recommended. Rods, reels and life jackets can all be rented.日焼け対策、雨具、飲食物（船内に冷蔵庫・レンジあり）、釣った魚を持ち帰るクーラー。氷は無料です。酔い止めは前夜と当日朝の服用がおすすめ。

A 30% deposit by card secures your booking (this demo simulates Stripe). The balance is settled on board — cash, credit cards (VISA/JCB/AMEX) and QR payments like PayPay are all accepted.予約時にカードで30%のデポジット（本デモではStripe決済を模擬）、残額は当日精算。現金・クレジットカード・QR決済に対応しています。

Safety first: if the captain cancels for weather, you get a full refund of your deposit or a free date change — your choice.船長判断で欠航の場合、デポジットは全額返金または日程変更を選べます。
